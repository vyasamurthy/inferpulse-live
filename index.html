The Core Metric

Workload Economic Index

WEI is the ratio of value your AI produces to what it costs. Every AIM gets a WEI score every 5 seconds. Below 1.0, you're losing money. Above 1.2, you're in the Scale zone.

1.4+
SCALE — increase GPU allocation
Value production significantly exceeds cost. These AIMs are your most profitable workloads.
1.0+
MAINTAIN — monitor weekly
Stable positive economics. No action required. Watch for drift that could push into Optimize.
0.6+
OPTIMIZE — investigate root cause
Declining WEI. Behavioral drift or SLA breach is eroding value. Remediation recommended.
<0.6
KILL — suspend to recover GPU spend
Negative economics. Every hour this AIM runs, you lose money. Suspend or reconfigure immediately.
